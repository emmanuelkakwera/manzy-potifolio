Personal introduction

I build useful things and make them look good.

I am an aspiring Computer Scientist with a passion for building practical technology solutions and creating meaningful digital experiences.

My journey in technology started with curiosity about how software works and has grown into hands-on experience across software development, web development, machine learning and IT support. I enjoy taking a real problem, understanding the people affected by it, and then designing a solution that is simple to use.

Beyond code, I work as a graphic designer, DJ and visual storyteller. That creative side shapes how I approach interfaces, branding and user experience. Whether I am training a text-classification model, designing a company profile, or mixing music at an event, the goal is the same: create something people remember.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at DMI St. John the Baptist University, with professional interests in Software Development, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Networking and Web Development.
